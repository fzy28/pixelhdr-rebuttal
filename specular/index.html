Relighting specular and metallic materials

Additional results for the question on highly specular, mirror-like materials. Every result on this page comes from our model trained only on synthetic data. For each input we show all lighting conditions that were run, without selection.

  • Input: the single source image given to the model
  • Target lighting: the target environment map (equirectangular panorama)
  • Our output: the relit image predicted by our model
  • Ground truth: the real capture under the target lighting (last part only)

Click any image to enlarge it. Inputs and outputs are the full square images used by the model, without cropping, except for the four-sphere scene, where each sphere is shown as its own crop.
